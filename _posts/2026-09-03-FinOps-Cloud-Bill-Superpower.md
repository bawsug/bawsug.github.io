FinOps: Turn your cloud bill from a mystery novel into a strategic superpower!

We're pulling back the curtain on your FinOps — and you'll never look at billing data the same way again.

- Say goodbye to billing chaos — hello, customized tracking by PO, account, or team
- Tagging workshops that finally answer: "Wait… who owns this resource?"
- Data pipeline magic with AWS Cost and Usage Reports — because raw data deserves better
- The game-changing metric: cost-per-customer (know exactly what you're spending and why)

Bottom line, we're handing you the blueprint to level up your governance, build a culture where everyone thinks like a FinOps pro, and maximize every dollar of your AWS investment. This isn't just a session — it's your team's cloud finance glow-up.

## Who Should Attend

This session is for both technical and business people. FinOps sits at the intersection of engineering, architecture, operations, finance, and business strategy.

No sales. No pitches. Just shared learning, practical thinking, and open discussion.

You should attend if you are a:

- **Cloud & Infrastructure Engineer** — working in AWS environments where AI tools, automation, and data access are rapidly expanding
- **Security, GRC, or Compliance Professional** — responsible for managing risk, governance, and regulatory alignment
- **Engineering Leader or Architect** — making design decisions that impact security, scale, and long-term resilience
- **CTO, CIO, or Technical Decision-Maker** — balancing innovation speed with risk, cost, and organizational accountability
- **Developer or Platform Team member** — using or experimenting with generative AI tools, APIs, copilots, or automation
- **Risk, Privacy, or Data Leader** — concerned about breach exposure, data usage, and regulatory impact as AI adoption grows
- Anyone interested in learning more about AWS
