Entrepreneurs, start-ups, and small businesses — pitch your startup at one of two upcoming events along the Colorado Front Range! Applications are now open for the **Startup & Small Business AI Pitch Competition**, sponsored by Futran Solutions, in partnership with AWS.

This pitch contest is an opportunity for small and growing businesses to showcase their innovative ideas and compete for a **$25,000 Development Grant** provided by Futran Solutions, in partnership with AWS.

## Event Details

**Boulder Event**
- **Date:** June 4, 2026
- **Location:** Colorado Quantum Incubator, Boulder, CO
- **Application Deadline:** May 28, 2026

**Golden Event**
- **Date:** June 25, 2026
- **Location:** Beck Venture Center, Golden, CO
- **Application Deadline:** June 12, 2026

## What to Expect

Selected participants will pitch live, receive expert feedback, and connect with investors, advisors, and AWS specialists. **10 companies will be invited to pitch at each event!**

Please refer to the [Business Plan Template](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RHQjU3icObtdjJY023bzCbggKvNKPxoO/view) for your attached summary.
