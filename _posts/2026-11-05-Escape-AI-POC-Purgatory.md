## Speakers

**Bani Sharma**

Bani Sharma is a Senior Solutions Architect at AWS, where she partners with FSI-payments industry customers to design secure, scalable and cost-efficient cloud architectures. She works across the AWS stack, from containers to data and analytics, and helps teams apply generative AI and agentic workloads with Amazon Bedrock and AgentCore to move ideas from proof of concept to production.

**Nikhil Karnik**

Nikhil Karnik is an AWS Solutions Architect partnering with Accenture across US East. He designs secure, scalable public sector architectures for benefits eligibility, child care, and Amazon Connect contact centers, and applies generative AI with Amazon Bedrock to modernize legacy systems and improve citizen services.
